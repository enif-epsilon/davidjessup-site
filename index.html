About the Author

David M. Jessup is the author of the Mariano trilogy — Mariano's Crossing, Mariano's Choice, and Mariano's Woman — three novels set in nineteenth-century Colorado that bring the real historical figure Mariano Medina and his world to life. A lifelong history buff with deep roots in the Colorado front range, Jessup blends careful research with a storyteller's instinct for character, weaving in the region's land and rivers — and his own long involvement in their conservation — as a quiet undercurrent through his writing life.
